What each check maps to — NANDA DataFacts

Authentic
One structural address for the object — re-encodings collapse, tampering still shows.
Current
A signed, ledger-anchored attestation you can prove after the fact.
Authorized
Capability chain checked live — and revocable before settlement.
Action
Verify → settle, reproducible; the verdict is signed so others can re-check.
DataFacts says what evidence exists. UOR makes it hold — and makes the agent's action accountable.
